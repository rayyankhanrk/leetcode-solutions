# Two Sum

**Difficulty:** Easy

**LeetCode:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

## Approach

Use two loops to check every pair of numbers. If their sum equals the target, return their indexes.

## Time Complexity

O(n²)

## Space Complexity

O(1)

## Test Cases

1. Input: nums = [2,7,11,15], target = 9  
   Output: [0,1]

2. Input: nums = [3,2,4], target = 6  
   Output: [1,2]

## Notes

The solution checks all possible pairs and works for typical and edge cases.
